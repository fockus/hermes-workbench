# Writing clearly / Понятный текст

Find out who will use the text and what they need to decide. Put the answer where they first look. Use topic headings when readers will return to the document. Keep exact commands, measurements, dates and qualifications. A shorter sentence is not an improvement if it loses the condition that makes it true.

For an edit, keep the author's meaning and register. Remove claims the source does not support; ask for missing facts instead of supplying plausible numbers. State a failure in terms of what happened, what was affected and what the reader can do next.
